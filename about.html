About Rosco

Decorating, done by someone who has to live with the result too.

Rosco Painting & Decorating Services is a small, hands-on decorating business built on one idea: a paint job should look right close-up, not just from across the room — and the house should be left as clean as it was found.

The approach

Tidy by default, not as an upsell.

Dust sheets go down before anything else does. Sanding runs through extraction equipment rather than spreading fine dust through every room. Tools and materials stay organised on site rather than sprawling across the house — small habits that add up to a much easier week for whoever lives there.

Every job starts with a proper estimate visit — no quoting blind from a photo, no inflated "trade" pricing. What's agreed at the start is what gets invoiced at the end.

  • Virtually dust-free sanding

    Extraction-fitted sanders capture fine dust at the source, so prep doesn't mean re-cleaning the whole house afterwards.

  • Honest, written estimates

    A clear price before work starts, based on an in-person look at the job — not a guess over the phone.

  • Careful prep, proper materials

    Surfaces are filled, sanded and primed properly before a top coat goes anywhere near them — quality trade paints throughout.

  • One point of contact

    You speak to Ross from the first estimate to the final walkthrough — no call centre, no subcontracted surprises.
