The Rosco difference

Sanding that doesn't take over your house.

Conventional sanding sends fine dust into every room — carpets, curtains, kitchen surfaces, lungs. Our extraction-fitted sanders pull dust away as it's made, so prep stays virtually dust-free and clean-up takes minutes, not days.

How a job runs

Four stages, one tidy site throughout.

01 — Estimate

Walk the job

A free visit to scope the rooms or surfaces, talk through finishes, and give an honest, written price.

02 — Prep

Dust-free sand & fill

Surfaces are sanded with extraction equipment, cracks filled, and everything masked off properly.

03 — Paint

Coat & finish

Quality trade paints applied in even coats — brushed, rolled or sprayed depending on the surface.

04 — Handover

Clean & walkthrough

Site cleaned, dust sheets gone, and a final walkthrough to check every edge and finish.
